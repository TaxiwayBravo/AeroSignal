STATION GUIDE

Ready for your receiver.

01 / Connect

Attach an RTL-SDR and a 1090 MHz antenna. Place the antenna high with a clear view of the sky.

02 / Configure

In the AeroSignal folder on your Pi, run:

python3 scripts/configure.py

Enter the antenna location, altitude in metres, and the platforms you want to feed.

03 / Start

bash scripts/start.sh

Open this dashboard on port 8080 and tar1090 on port 8081. Give the receiver a few minutes to collect traffic.

04 / Keep up to date

bash scripts/update.sh

For a Git clone, this pulls a fast-forward update from your configured GitHub branch, then refreshes the containers. Your local configuration stays on the Pi.

Designed for your trusted local network. Do not port-forward the dashboard or receiver interfaces to the internet. See README.md for hardware support, account setup, recovery, and troubleshooting.
